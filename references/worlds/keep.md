# World VII — The Endless Keep (finale)

**Brief.** The Nightwright's fortress, made from places his Heralds folded away. It draws on the *feeling* of Infinity Castle: endless lamp-lit halls, rooms at every angle, dizzying verticality, and ufotable's goal of a near-infinite 3D space ([Popverse interview with DoP Yūichi Terao](https://www.thepopverse.com/movies-demon-slayer-kimetsu-no-yaiba-yuichi-terao-interview-making-the-infinity-castle-feel-infinite)). Its tiered atrium takes its cue from multi-storey onsen ryokan atria such as Ookawaso (Aizu Ashinomaki), which fans link to the film. Every layout, motif and name here is original: red and black lacquer, tatami, warm shoji, and the brass Keeper fittings that echo the Folded Court in World I. It uses no characters, logos or copied maps.

**Construction.** `parts.js` bakes a few prototypes once: a tatami room (detailed and lite LODs), a sliding shoji door, a stair, a gallery, a lantern and a hip roof. These are drawn as `InstancedMesh` thousands of times. `facing(pos, normal, roll)` sets a room's open front on any wall, ceiling or floor and rolls it, so the rooms are upright, upside down or sideways. Doors slide in `update()` through per-instance matrices. Additive halo points (`halos()`) stand in for most lamp light, and each region uses at most 8 PointLights, none of them casting shadows. Exp2 fog fades the repetition, so it reads as endless.

**Review pass (coordinator).** In the Halls, walls are now irregular stacks with per-column scale, depth, roll and tilt, plus gaps and overhanging eaves. Diagonal bridges and stair flights cross the void at many heights. Depth grading runs from warm near tiers through dim and red tiers to a near-black `deepPaper` far above and below. The inverted pagoda is lowered, warm-lit, and visible from spawn. In the Meridian, fragments are warm-lit miniatures on crumbling, paper-edged earth chunks. The horizon is a ring of 620 roofed, lit buildings (three house types) at many heights. The crease now has a real additive light curtain: the old crease rendered but sat 4 cm high and 0.3 m wide, so at eye height it read as one more brass line. In the Atrium, the crown grid is replaced by 18 square frames that twist upward into fog, and the tiers rise to 16.

**Known limits.** Moving architecture is decorative, and every walkable route is fixed. The world has no gravity-flip traversal yet. Orbiting fragments are simplified silhouettes. No Atlas generation was used: everything is code geometry plus the existing local textures and canvas-painted fusuma and backboard.
